1. Measure camera projection

Hold the camera level. Place a measured vertical reference centered in the full image, perpendicular to the optical axis. Measure its axial distance from the camera lens (not a slanted tape distance). Freeze the image, then mark its top and bottom. Do not change lens, zoom, resolution or height afterward.

Tap both reference endpoints above, or enter their positions below (0% = image top). The reference must be vertically centered.

2. Select a ground-contact point

Tap the live depth image to place the crosshair at a visible ground-contact point. This is not an automatic distance to a detected object. Keep the selected point on flat, level ground.

Intervals describe optical-axis depth, not nearest-obstacle clearance. Bounds assume ±2° motion error and measured tolerances, not a validated confidence probability. Stop and recalibrate after any camera-height or projection change.
