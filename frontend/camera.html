HOW IT WORKS

About this demo

Walkthrough: Add a backpack, move it out of the marked area, or remove it. The demo sends an alert after the object appears in two checks.

Live camera: Clarity sends small camera images to your backend to check for selected objects. The backend decides whether to send an alert. Images are processed but not saved.

Clarity can miss hazards such as curbs, stairs, potholes, and barriers. The marked area does not measure distance or show whether a path is clear.

Scanning stops when you leave this page. Background scanning is not part of this demo.
