A SECOND LOOK

Here’s what you’re trying.

Walkthrough uses an illustrated scene and simulated detections. Add a backpack, move it out of the marked zone, or remove it. Two matching observations produce an alert.

Live camera sends small JPEG frames to your running backend. Its pretrained model returns supported objects and decides whether an alert should fire. Frames are processed without being saved.

The image zone is a prototype heuristic, not a distance measurement. This model recognizes people, chairs, bags, and other selected objects; it does not recognize every hazard, including curbs, stairs, or potholes.

Scanning pauses when you leave this page. This browser sample does not demonstrate background scanning behind Instagram.
